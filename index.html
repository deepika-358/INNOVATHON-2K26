About the event

About INNOVATHON 2K26

INNOVATHON 2K26 is an innovation challenge designed to encourage students to identify real-world problems, develop innovative ideas, build a prototype, test their solution, and present their work before a panel of judges.

Innovation

Fresh thinking applied to real problems worth solving.

Creativity

Original approaches, not just textbook solutions.

Problem Solving

Structured thinking under a tight, real event clock.

Prototyping

Turning an idea into something you can hold and show.

Teamwork

3–4 members working as one unit through the day.

Practical Implementation

Working prototypes over paper ideas alone.

Presentation Skills

Pitching your work clearly to a panel of judges.
